Peas and Rice

Back To The Homepage

Ingredients:

  • 100g Peas
  • 60g Rice
  • Soysauce
  • Bacon Bits (optional)
  • 1 egg (optional)

Steps:

  1. Cook the rice and the peas
  2. if you have the egg or bacon, bake that as you normally would
  3. when the peas and the rice is ready add it together
  4. add your bacon or egg to taste
  5. add soysauce to taste
