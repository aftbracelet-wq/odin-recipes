Pastasauce

Ingredients:

  • 6 large onions
  • 2/3 bellpeppers according to taste
  • 2 zuchinis
  • 1 large carrot
  • 2 cans of diced tomatoes
  • 2 cans of blended tomatoes
  • 1 small can of tomato paste
  • 6 cloves of garlic

Steps:

  1. dice all your vegetables, except the carrots which you grate
  2. add the diced onions to a large saucepan
  3. let simmer till yellow
  4. add the diced bellpepers
  5. stir for 5 minutes
  6. add the diced zuchini and the grated carrot
  7. after a few minutes add all the canned tomatoes and the garlic
  8. season to taste
