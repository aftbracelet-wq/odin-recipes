Mugcake

Back To The Homepage

Ingredients:

  • 1 tbsp cocoapowder
  • 4 tbsp flour
  • 6 tbsp milk
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 1/4 tsp baking soda
  • 2 tbsp granulated sugar

Steps:

  1. Add everything together
  2. Stir well, watch the borders of the mug
  3. place it in the microwave
  4. on 700 watt, wait for 80 seconds
